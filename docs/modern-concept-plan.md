# Modern concept direction

A second design concept will live at `/modern/` while the current site remains unchanged at `/`.

The concept will emphasize:

- Lowcountry and Lady's Island location context
- Family-friendly, modern visual design
- Strong call, directions, forms, and general-inquiry actions
- Dr. William Donovan's approved biography and photograph
- Clearly marked sample team-role content for front desk, hygiene, and dental assisting
- A safe general-inquiry experience that warns users not to submit protected health information
- No patient intake upload, no medical-history submission, and no production email delivery until the practice approves a recipient and workflow

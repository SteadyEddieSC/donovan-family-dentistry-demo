# Provider Pricing Source Notes

Checked July 31, 2026. These notes support the comparison in `service-provider-options.md`.

- Cloudflare Turnstile: Free plan with up to 20 widgets and unlimited challenges.
- Cloudflare Web Analytics: free privacy-oriented analytics.
- Cloudflare Workers: free tier; paid plan has a $5 monthly minimum.
- Cloudflare Pages: static asset requests are free and unlimited; Functions use Workers quotas.
- Basin: Free plan includes 1 form and 50 submissions/month; Starter is $12.50/month billed annually; Growth is $24.17/month billed annually; Pro is listed at $40.83/month before temporary promotions.
- Jotform Gold: $99/month billed annually and includes HIPAA-enabled features.
- Hushmail Secure Messaging & Forms: $14.99/month displayed for one user and up to 25 forms; BAA included.
- Spruce: Basic $24/user/month and Communicator $49/user/month; a $19.50 outbound SMS registration fee and telecom charges may apply.
- Weave: official pages currently show starting figures of $149 and $199/month; request a written dental-practice quote.
- NexHealth and LocalMed: quote required.
- Formstack Forms: starts at $83/month annual or $99/month monthly; confirm healthcare plan and BAA terms.
- OhMD: Core starts at $300/month and AI & Automation starts at $500/month.

Before purchase, confirm contract term, implementation fee, BAA, cancellation terms, SMS/phone usage, number porting, practice-management integration, support, data export, retention, and deletion.

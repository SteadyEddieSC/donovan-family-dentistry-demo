# Owner content request for Release 12

Release 12 will replace fictional and unverified demo material with approved practice content. Partial information is acceptable; unconfirmed sections can be removed rather than published inaccurately.

## Associate dentist

- Full name and preferred public name
- Degree and credentials exactly as they should appear
- Approved biography
- Education and professional affiliations that may be published
- Clinical interests, avoiding unsupported specialty claims
- Approved headshot or practice photograph
- Preferred pronouns, only if the provider wants them published

## Practice team

For each person the practice wants shown:

- Full name or approved first-name-only format
- Role/title
- Short biography or patient-facing description
- Approved photograph

The website does not need to name every team member. A role-based team section can replace individual profiles when preferred.

## Practice history and identity

- Year the practice opened
- Founder/ownership history that may be published
- Major office or community milestones
- What the practice wants patients to know about its Lady's Island and Beaufort identity
- Any wording that should be avoided

## Services

Confirm whether each currently listed service remains offered and whether the terminology should change. Identify any services to add or remove. Do not describe a provider as a specialist unless that designation is accurate and approved.

## New-patient and office policies

- Whether the practice is accepting new patients
- Recommended arrival time
- Required forms and identification
- Dental-record and X-ray transfer instructions
- Insurance-participation wording
- Payment methods and financing options
- Cancellation, rescheduling, and late-arrival wording
- Urgent dental and after-hours instructions
- Conditions that should be directed to 911 or an emergency department

## Images

Useful approved images include:

- Exterior and entrance
- Reception and waiting area
- Treatment rooms
- Dentists and team
- Lowcountry or Lady's Island details connected to the actual practice

Provide the highest-resolution originals available and confirm that the practice has permission to publish them.

## Administrative inquiry workflow

For the later activation release, identify:

- The mailbox or team responsible for website inquiries
- Expected response time
- Who handles missed or failed messages
- How long administrative messages should be retained
- Whether the practice wants web analytics
- Whether the current phone number can receive text messages

Do not send patient medical details, insurance identifiers, Social Security numbers, or treatment records through ordinary email for this content-collection process.
